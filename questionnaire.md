# Vendor Security Questionnaire

1. Is your organization certified against ISO/IEC 27001 or another recognized information security standard? If yes, please provide evidence of certification.

2. How do you protect sensitive customer information during storage and transmission? Please describe your encryption methods.

3. Is Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) required for privileged or administrative accounts?

4. How often do you perform vulnerability assessments and penetration testing on your systems?

5. What is your incident response process, and within what timeframe will customers be notified following a confirmed security incident?

6. Do you perform employee background checks for personnel who have access to customer systems or sensitive information?

7. How do you manage software updates, security patches, and vulnerability remediation within your environment?

8. Do you use subcontractors or fourth-party suppliers to deliver any part of your services? If yes, how are their security practices assessed?

9. What procedures are followed to securely return or permanently delete customer information when a contract or service agreement ends?

10. Can you provide recent audit reports, compliance certifications, penetration testing summaries, or other evidence demonstrating the effectiveness of your security controls?
